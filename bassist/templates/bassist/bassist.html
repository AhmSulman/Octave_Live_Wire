Try: walking, funk, reggae, rock, jazz, play in E minor, busier / calmer, build up, give me a fill, crash, half-time / double-time, swing it / straight, faster / slower, louder / quieter, more fills / no fills, drop the drums / bass. Chain them with commas.
How to jam ▾
  1. Enable the mic and allow access. Use your guitar's pickup into an audio interface, or just play acoustically near the mic.
  2. Hit Start Jam. The bassist starts on a count and locks to the BPM you set.
  3. In Follow mode, play a note or strum a chord — the bot hears the root and re-roots the bass on the next bar. Play single notes / clear roots for the most reliable tracking.
  4. Prefer to drive it yourself? Switch to Manual key and tap a root — the bass vamps there until you change it.
  5. Pick a bass style and chord color to match your tune, dial in swing, and turn drums on for a fuller backing.
  6. Latch keeps the last chord ringing when you pause, so the groove never drops out mid-jam.
